Virtual Harp Accompaniment Playing System
Hello, this is my project, Virtual Harp. You can try playing strings to create sounds like harp glissandos or plucking. Here are the steps:

1. Prepare a webcam and position it about 1 meter away at your front-right, at neck height.

2. Open the following URL and make sure your head and upper body are visible in the browser's camera preview.

3. You will see yourself with 7 strings in front of you if the detection is successful.

4. Choose a song from the list (on the left side of the screen).

5. Imagine yourself holding a harp in front of you.
   Try sweeping both hands alternately, similar to playing a real harp.

6. You will see visual feedback and hear harp synth sounds as soon as the singer sings.

Try it out, play, and enjoy!
